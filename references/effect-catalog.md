## Composition

Combine at most two primary motion ideas in one viewport. Good pairs separate a base state from an interaction or transition:

- `nebula-breathing` + `gravity-lensing`
- `pattern-convergence` + `wave-propagation`
- `orbital-system` + `chromatic-migration`
- `stellar-rivers` + `stardust-descent`

Avoid combining two effects that both control global particle position, such as `galaxy-vortex` and `celestial-fold`, unless the transition explicitly blends between them.
