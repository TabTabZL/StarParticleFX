# 时间倒流 / Time reversal

## Visual contract

Scattered debris retraces its history and reconstructs a previous star field or object. Reverse motion should read as recovery, not an ordinary inward collapse.

## Motion model

Record or deterministically reconstruct source trajectories, then evaluate them with reversed normalized time. Preserve asymmetric drag trails, rotation, and stagger order so the reverse retains evidence of its forward history.

## Parameters

`reverseProgress`, `trajectoryDuration`, `stagger`, `trailLength`, `targetState`, `holdAfterRestore`.

Use exact reversible paths when continuity matters. Avoid substituting simple radial attraction. Reduced motion shows the restored state.
