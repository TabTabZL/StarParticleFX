# 星辰冻结 / Stellar freeze

## Visual contract

Moving particles lose energy, lock into precise positions, and grow crystalline connections. The final state should feel engineered rather than merely paused.

## Motion model

Blend from free particle positions to a hexagonal, radial, or target-derived lattice. Reduce velocity and twinkle before revealing short crystalline edges. Introduce the freeze boundary as a traveling front rather than freezing every particle simultaneously.

## Parameters

`freezeProgress`, `frontPosition`, `latticeType`, `cellSize`, `snapStrength`, `edgeReveal`, `crystalHue`.

Avoid a perfect full-screen grid; keep seeded defects and depth. Reduced motion renders the frozen state.
