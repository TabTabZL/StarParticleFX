# 星辰分裂 / Stellar division

## Visual contract

Seed stars duplicate into increasingly complex local families, suggesting growth and evolution without uncontrolled screen-filling noise.

## Motion model

Assign particles to a parent seed, generation, branch angle, and delay. Reveal generations sequentially and expand children along short curved paths before they join the ambient field. Reuse a fixed pool instead of allocating particles during animation.

## Parameters

`generationCount`, `branchFactor`, `generationDelay`, `spread`, `childScale`, `growthProgress`, `poolSize`.

Cap both generations and visible density. Give older generations lower alpha. Reduced motion shows a balanced mature cluster.
