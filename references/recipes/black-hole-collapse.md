# 黑洞坍缩 / Black-hole collapse

## Visual contract

The field stretches, accelerates, spirals, and disappears into a compact center. A dark event-horizon core should remain visible until the final compression.

## Motion model

Drive radius with a nonlinear collapse curve while increasing twist inversely with radius. Stretch particles tangentially or along velocity only during the acceleration phase. Remove or recycle particles after crossing the event horizon.

## Parameters

`collapseProgress`, `eventHorizon`, `twist`, `radialExponent`, `streakLength`, `center`, `holdAtSingularity`.

Avoid a uniformly shrinking cloud. Preserve radial timing differences and a dark core. Reduced motion shows the pre-collapse accretion state.
