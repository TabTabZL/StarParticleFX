# 波浪传播 / Wave propagation

## Visual contract

A visible energy front crosses the field; each star displaces, brightens, and returns in sequence. The wave should communicate direction and causality.

## Motion model

Compute signed distance from each particle to a circular, linear, or path-based wavefront. Use a narrow band function for displacement and brightness, followed by a damped trailing oscillation.

## Parameters

`origin`, `direction`, `speed`, `wavelength`, `amplitude`, `bandWidth`, `damping`, `highlight`.

Keep the leading edge narrow and the recovery smooth. Trigger from a meaningful event or scroll threshold. Reduced motion displays the wave at a representative midpoint without oscillation.
